The platform begins by organising operational data into a consistent client structure, allowing multiple datasets to be validated and processed together.

The implementation is intentionally modular. Independent components handle ingestion, diagnostics and reporting, while reusable execution scripts orchestrate the complete pipeline.

This architecture also allows individual diagnostic domains to run independently. Organisations can execute targeted reviews, such as Leave or Termination diagnostics, or combine multiple domains into a comprehensive operational assessment.

Every execution produces a structured collection of outputs, ranging from detailed module findings through to executive reports designed for governance and operational review.
