Payroll systems are excellent at processing transactions. But they're rarely designed to identify hidden operational risks before they become audits, employee disputes, or compliance investigations.

I built the Payroll Diagnostics Engine as an independent operational diagnostics platform that sits above existing payroll and HR systems.

Rather than replacing an existing payroll platform, its role is to independently analyse operational data, identify potential risks, and produce evidence-backed findings that support further investigation.

The engineering objective wasn't to calculate payroll. It was to build an independent diagnostics layer capable of detecting operational risks that traditional payroll systems were never designed to identify.

To achieve that, I designed the platform around a layered architecture. Operational datasets are first ingested, validated, and standardised before any diagnostics begin.

The core of the platform is a modular rule engine. Each diagnostic domain executes independently, analysing a different aspect of operational risk before contributing evidence to a unified assessment.

Those findings are then consolidated, classified by severity, and transformed into executive-ready reports that help organisations investigate issues before they become larger operational or compliance problems.
