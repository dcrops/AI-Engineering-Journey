Paying employees correctly for public holidays sounds straightforward, but the applicable entitlement depends on much more than an employee's state.

This application evaluates both office and home work locations independently to determine which public holiday calendar applies.

In this example, the office location qualifies for thirteen public holidays, while the home location qualifies for fifteen, demonstrating how regional rules can produce different payroll outcomes for different work locations.

Each address is resolved into geographic coordinates and matched against Australian Bureau of Statistics local government boundaries before the relevant holiday rules are applied.

Every result includes the applicable public holidays, the confidence of the geographic match and a complete audit trail, making every decision transparent and easy to validate.

While evaluating a single employee is useful for investigating individual cases, payroll teams often need to assess an entire workforce.

The same decision engine can therefore be applied to batch processing, where every employee is evaluated independently using the same geographic resolution and holiday rule evaluation pipeline.

Rather than returning technical results alone, the application also generates a professional compliance report designed for operational review and audit activities.

An executive summary highlights the overall findings, while supporting sections explain the review methodology, applicable public holiday calendars and the severity of any identified issues.

Records requiring attention are prioritised using risk-based classifications, with detailed evidence provided for every finding, including the employee, resolved location, applicable public holidays and the reasoning behind the result.

By combining executive summaries with detailed supporting evidence, the report allows payroll teams to understand not only what was identified, but why each conclusion was reached, creating a transparent and fully auditable review process.
